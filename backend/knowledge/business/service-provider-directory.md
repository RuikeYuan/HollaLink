# Local Service Provider Directory (Lawyers / Accountants / Commercial Real Estate Brokers)

> **Important note (the advisor agent must follow this strictly)**: the organizations below were found through public research and **actually exist and can be independently verified** — they are not partners contracted or certified by the "Dutch Business Navigator" platform, and this platform has not independently verified the quality of their services. ## Law firms (immigration law / business law, with Chinese-language service)
- **Everaert Advocaten** — has a China Desk, founded in 1982, specializes in serving Chinese companies entering the European market via the Netherlands; handles residence permits, work permits, and company formation advice (everaert.nl)
- **Jing Law Firm** — founded by Jing Guo, a Chinese lawyer practicing in the Netherlands; handles commercial litigation and M&A/investment matters (jinglawfirm.nl)
- **HULX** (The Hague) — has a Chinese-language department (hulx.nl)
- **Pieters Advocaten** (Utrecht) — has a China affairs department; handles immigration law and employment law (pietersadvocaten.cn)
- **Q Advocaten** (Rotterdam) — offers a Chinese-language page (qadvocaten.nl)

## Accounting / tax service providers
- **Neplus Financial Service** — explicitly serves Chinese businesses in the Netherlands and Chinese-invested companies based there; offers company registration, tax number/bank account applications, VAT filing, and cross-border e-commerce VAT registration (neplusfs.com)

## Official/systematic lookup channels (more comprehensive than ad-hoc searching)
- **NOvA (Dutch Bar Association) official lookup system** — filter registered lawyers nationwide by practice area (e.g. vreemdelingenrecht/immigration law, ondernemingsrecht/business law), covering far more firms than the list above
- **NBA (Royal Netherlands Institute of Chartered Accountants) / RB (Register Belastingadviseurs) official registries** — likewise more systematic than ad-hoc searching
- **IND official list of certified facilitators** — if the client is considering the Startup Visa route, the IND website publishes an official list of certified facilitator organizations, whose entire business is guiding foreign entrepreneurs

## Commercial brokers / horeca-specialist real estate agents
For restaurant/retail site selection, prioritize a **horeca-specialist broker** over a general residential real estate agent — specialist brokers better understand zoning verification, business transfers (bedrijfsovername), and other industry norms:
- **Adhoc Horecamakelaars** — horeca lease/sale specialist broker in the Amsterdam area (adhoc-horecamakelaars.nl)
- **Van Alkemare Horecamakelaardij** — covers horeca sale/lease/valuation across North Holland, South Holland, Utrecht, and Flevoland (vanalkemare.nl)
- **Dam Horecamakelaar / Klaassen Horecamakelaars** — horeca commercial brokers in the Amsterdam area
- **Horecasite.nl / Bedrijfspand.com** — nationwide horeca business/premises transfer listing platforms, with over 2,200 horeca businesses listed for sale/transfer; when looking to "take over an existing business" (see [[cases/case-001-hotpot-ventilation.md|the real state of Chinese restaurants]]), start with platforms like these
